I was hired by [Seasoned.cc](https://seasoned.cc) to work on the [Vibe](https://www.metadados.com.br/vibe) project, a product from Metadados, an HR company focused on making HR management easier. Vibe was built using React and Typescript.

I worked in a small team — me, two devs, a project owner and a designer — and soon moved to a tech lead position, handling not only deliverables but also critical architecture decisions and team integration.
