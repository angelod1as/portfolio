[Plutão Books](https://www.plutaolivros.com.br/)

Bringing together all issues of the satirical newspaper [Tempos Fantásticos](/projects/tempos-fantasticos), this anthology explores speculative fiction through unlikely futures, alternative pasts, and surreal presents. With opinion columns, imaginary reports, comics, illustrations, and ads, the collection blends creativity and humor across science fiction, fantasy, horror, and history. The History of Tempos Fantásticos celebrates inventive storytelling that transforms the everyday into the extraordinary.
