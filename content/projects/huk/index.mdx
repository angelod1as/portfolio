HUK-Autoservice is a digital take on services provided by [HUK-Autowelt](https://www.huk-autowelt.de/), a big car insurance company in Germany. The web app offers for the user an improved experience of booking multiple services for their vehicles, taking advantage of a diverse chain of partner garages. It was built using **Next.JS** (**React** and **Typescript**).

I was hired by German company [foobar Agency](https://foobar.agency) to work as a Frontend developer on the HUK-Autowelt project, and worked closely for circa 2 years with a a full-fledged product team, from frontend development to DevOps to marketing. With the project's progress, my role went from simple contributor to frontend lead, working alongside the tech lead, making architectural decisions, and developing other professionals.
