## I was

- a consultant and developer building the [HUK Autoservice](/projects/huk) storefront in [foobar Agency
GmbH](https://foobar.agency/), in Munich, Germany;
- a tech lead of a small team on [Seasoned.cc](https://www.seasoned.cc/), working alongside designers and
developers to build the HR web app [Vibe](https://www.metadados.com.br/vibe);
- a designer and front-end developer at Latin America's biggest newspaper, [Folha de
S.Paulo](https://www.folha.uol.com.br/). I designed print and digital pages, built multimedia infographics, and
developed internal and external digital tools. At Folha I worked on a project that won Brazil's biggest journalism
award, among other prizes;
- a writer, editor, and manager in the satirical sci-fi newspaper [Tempos
Fantásticos](/projects/tempos-fantasticos) for 28 editions (3 years);
- a programmer of [a box that prints](/projects/poem-printer) poems, short stories, and
comic strips in collaboration with an LGBTQIA+ house in São Paulo, Brazil;
- the scriptwriter of [Combo Breaker](/projects/combo-breaker), a silent story written by me and drawn by
Brazilian comic artist Silva João;
- an amateur [solo music maker](https://soundcloud.com/angelod1as).
