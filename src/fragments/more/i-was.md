## I was

- a writer, editor, and manager in the satirical sci-fi newspaper [Tempos
  Fantásticos](/projects/tempos-fantasticos) for 28 editions (3 years);
- a programmer of [a box that prints](/projects/recibo) poems, short stories, and
  comic strips in collaboration with an LGBTQIA+ house in São Paulo, Brazil;
- the scriptwriter of [Combo Breaker](/projects/combo-breaker), a silent story written by me and drawn by
  Brazilian comic artist Silva João;
- an amateur [solo music maker](https://soundcloud.com/angelod1as).
